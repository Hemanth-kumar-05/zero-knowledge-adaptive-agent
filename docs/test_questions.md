# RAG Agent Test Questions - Phase 1

## ✅ Questions the Agent SHOULD Answer (25)

### Academic Advising & Mentorship
**Source:** `academic_advising_and_mentorship.md`

1. **Q:** What is the primary role of a Faculty Advisor at NCIE?
   - **Expected:** Guide course selection, monitor progress, coordinate support for academic concerns

2. **Q:** When are Faculty Advisors assigned to students?
   - **Expected:** At the beginning of the academic program or academic year

3. **Q:** Can Faculty Advisors replace course instructors or evaluation authorities?
   - **Expected:** No, Faculty Advisors do not replace course instructors or evaluation authorities

---

### Continuous Assessment
**Source:** `continuous_assessment_overview.md`

4. **Q:** What percentage of total course evaluation does Continuous Assessment contribute at NCIE?
   - **Expected:** 40% of total course evaluation

5. **Q:** What are the main components that may be included in Continuous Assessment?
   - **Expected:** Mid-semester tests, assignments, quizzes, laboratory performance, project reviews

6. **Q:** Is attendance linked to Continuous Assessment?
   - **Expected:** Yes, for certain courses, attendance may be considered as part of CA framework

7. **Q:** What happens if a student misses a Continuous Assessment activity?
   - **Expected:** Student should inform instructor/advisor, documentation may be requested, alternate assessment may be provided at department's discretion

---

### Course Add/Drop/Withdrawal
**Source:** `course_add_drop_and_withdrawal.md`

8. **Q:** What is the difference between dropping and withdrawing from a course?
   - **Expected:** Dropping removes course early in semester with no record; withdrawal happens later and is reflected distinctly in academic records

9. **Q:** Who reviews and approves course add requests at NCIE?
   - **Expected:** Faculty Advisor or Department Academic Coordinator

10. **Q:** Do dropped courses appear in the student's academic performance records?
    - **Expected:** No, dropped courses do not appear in academic performance records

11. **Q:** How does course withdrawal affect examination eligibility?
    - **Expected:** Withdrawn courses are not eligible for end-semester examination

---

### Exam Registration
**Source:** `exam_registration_process.md`

12. **Q:** What types of examinations require registration at NCIE?
    - **Expected:** End-Semester Examinations (ESE), Supplementary/Reappearance Examinations, Makeup Examinations

13. **Q:** How much does the End-Semester Examination contribute to course evaluation?
    - **Expected:** 60% of the total course evaluation

14. **Q:** Is exam registration required for Continuous Assessment components?
    - **Expected:** No, CA registration is handled implicitly through course enrollment

15. **Q:** What are the key eligibility checks performed before exam registration?
    - **Expected:** Active enrollment, completion of CA components, attendance records, no academic flags, examination fee completion

16. **Q:** Who announces the exam registration timeline?
    - **Expected:** The Examination Cell of NCIE

---

### Final Year Project
**Source:** `final_year_project_guidelines.md`

17. **Q:** Which semesters does the final-year project span?
    - **Expected:** Seventh and eighth semesters

18. **Q:** Can final-year projects be done individually or only in groups?
    - **Expected:** Either individually or in small student groups, as guided by the department

19. **Q:** What is the role of a Project Guide?
    - **Expected:** Academic supervision, progress monitoring, and evaluation input

20. **Q:** How is the final-year project evaluated?
    - **Expected:** Through scheduled reviews that form part of Continuous Assessment

21. **Q:** What are the key evaluation criteria for final-year projects?
    - **Expected:** Problem definition clarity, technical depth, progress consistency, documentation quality, presentation effectiveness

### Revaluation
**Source:** `revaluation_and_answer_script_review.md`

33. **Q:** What is the purpose of revaluation at NCIE?
    - **Expected:** Verify evaluation accuracy, address potential errors, provide academic reassurance

34. **Q:** Can students request revaluation for Continuous Assessment marks?
    - **Expected:** No, revaluation applies only to End-Semester Examinations (ESE)

35. **Q:** What types of review mechanisms are available?
    - **Expected:** Answer Script Review (verification of marks/totaling) and Revaluation (reassessment by evaluator)

36. **Q:** Who coordinates the revaluation process at NCIE?
    - **Expected:** The Examination Cell

37. **Q:** Can marks decrease after revaluation?
    - **Expected:** Marks may remain unchanged or be revised; updated marks replace previous records
