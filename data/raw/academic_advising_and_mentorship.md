## 1. Introduction

At **Nova Crest Institute of Engineering (NCIE)**, academic advising and mentorship form a structured support mechanism that guides students through their academic journey. Advising focuses on helping students understand academic processes, plan coursework, monitor progress, and address challenges that may arise during the program.

Academic advising at NCIE is integrated into the institutional academic framework and operates in coordination with course enrollment, assessment, examination, and project workflows.

## 2. Purpose of Academic Advising

The academic advising and mentorship framework at NCIE is designed to:

* Support informed academic decision-making
* Guide students in course planning and progression
* Assist in understanding academic workflows and expectations
* Identify and address academic or adjustment challenges early
* Provide continuity and support across semesters

Advising emphasizes guidance and coordination rather than evaluation.

## 3. Faculty Advisor Assignment

Each student at NCIE is assigned a **Faculty Advisor** at the beginning of the academic program or academic year. The Faculty Advisor serves as the primary academic point of contact for the student throughout the advising period.

Advisor assignments are recorded in the academic system and may be updated by the department when required.

## 4. Role of the Faculty Advisor

The Faculty Advisor at NCIE typically supports students by:

* Guiding course selection and enrollment adjustments
* Advising on add, drop, and withdrawal processes
* Monitoring academic progress across semesters
* Clarifying academic workflows and timelines
* Coordinating support for academic concerns

Faculty Advisors do not replace course instructors or evaluation authorities.

## 5. Advising Interactions and Communication

Academic advising interactions may occur through:

* Scheduled advisory meetings
* Departmental advising sessions
* Academic portal communication
* Informal consultations as needed

Advisory interactions may increase during key academic phases such as semester start, course adjustment periods, and examination preparation phases.

## 6. Advising in Course Enrollment and Adjustments

Faculty Advisors play an important role in enrollment-related processes by:

* Discussing academic workload feasibility
* Advising on elective selection
* Reviewing add, drop, or withdrawal requests
* Ensuring alignment with academic progression plans

Advisory input supports informed decisions but final actions are recorded through formal academic workflows.

## 7. Advising Related to Assessment and Performance

Advisors may assist students in understanding:

* Continuous Assessment components and expectations
* Integration of CA and End-Semester Examination outcomes
* Interpretation of academic performance records
* Steps available after result declaration, such as revaluation processes

Advisors help students navigate academic systems without intervening in evaluation decisions.

## 8. Mentorship During Projects and Internships

During project and internship phases, academic mentorship may include:

* Guidance on aligning activities with academic objectives
* Support in managing timelines and milestones
* Coordination with project guides or departmental coordinators
* Clarifying documentation and submission expectations

Mentorship complements, but does not replace, formal project supervision or internship evaluation mechanisms.

## 9. Identification and Support of Academic Challenges

Faculty Advisors may assist in identifying academic challenges such as:

* Consistent assessment difficulties
* Course overload concerns
* Academic planning uncertainties
* Adjustment issues consideration

When necessary, advisors coordinate with departments or academic offices to guide students toward appropriate support mechanisms.

## 10. Role of Departments in Advising Oversight

Departments at NCIE oversee academic advising by:

* Assigning and monitoring advisor responsibilities
* Ensuring advising consistency across student groups
* Supporting advisors with academic information and system access
* Coordinating interventions when needed

Departmental oversight ensures advising effectiveness and alignment with institutional practices.

## 11. Documentation and Record Keeping

Advising-related records at NCIE may include:

* Advisor assignments
* Advising session summaries, where applicable
* Enrollment adjustment approvals
* Academic progression notes

Records are maintained to support continuity and coordination across academic systems.

## 12. Advising Boundaries and Scope

Academic advising at NCIE focuses on guidance and coordination. It does not involve:

* Modification of assessment outcomes
* Override of evaluation decisions
* Independent alteration of academic records

Formal academic processes remain governed by established workflows.

## 13. System Integration

Academic advising processes are supported by integration with:

* Student academic profiles
* Course enrollment systems
* Assessment and examination records
* Project and internship tracking modules

System access enables advisors to provide informed and timely guidance.

## 14. Summary

At Nova Crest Institute of Engineering, academic advising and mentorship provide structured, continuous support for students across their academic lifecycle. Through coordinated guidance, clear communication, and system-enabled oversight, advising helps students navigate academic processes effectively while remaining aligned with institutional frameworks.
