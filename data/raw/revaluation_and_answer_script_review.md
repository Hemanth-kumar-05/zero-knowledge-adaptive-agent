## 1. Introduction

At **Nova Crest Institute of Engineering (NCIE)**, the revaluation and answer script review process provides students with a formal academic mechanism to request a reassessment or review of their evaluated examination scripts. This process supports transparency and confidence in the examination and evaluation system.

Revaluation and script review apply **only to End-Semester Examinations (ESE)** and are initiated after the declaration of examination results. This document outlines the workflow, roles involved, and academic practices associated with revaluation at NCIE.

## 2. Purpose of Revaluation and Script Review

The revaluation process at NCIE is intended to:

* Allow students to seek verification of evaluation accuracy
* Address potential errors in mark totaling or assessment
* Provide academic reassurance through structured review
* Maintain trust in the examination evaluation process

Revaluation is not intended to replace academic evaluation standards but to ensure correctness and consistency.

## 3. Eligibility for Revaluation Requests

Students may request revaluation or answer script review for courses in which:

* End-Semester Examination results have been officially declared
* The course evaluation includes a written ESE component
* The student has completed required examination registration

Continuous Assessment components are **not subject to revaluation** under this process.

## 4. Types of Review Mechanisms

NCIE supports the following academic review mechanisms:

* **Answer Script Review**
  Verification of mark allocation, totaling, and recording accuracy.

* **Revaluation**
  Reassessment of the answer script by an evaluator following institutional procedures.

The availability of these mechanisms may vary depending on academic scheduling and evaluation workflows.

## 5. Revaluation Request Workflow

The standard revaluation process at NCIE includes:

1. Student accesses the academic portal after result declaration.
2. Eligible courses are displayed for revaluation or review.
3. Student submits a request for the selected course(s).
4. Required details and acknowledgments are completed.
5. The request is recorded and forwarded to the Examination Cell.
6. Revaluation or review is conducted as per academic workflow.
7. Updated results, if any, are reflected in the academic system.

Requests submitted outside the designated request window may require additional verification.

## 6. Role of the Examination Cell

The **Examination Cell** at NCIE coordinates the revaluation process by:

* Managing revaluation request timelines
* Assigning answer scripts for review or reassessment
* Ensuring anonymity and integrity during evaluation
* Updating verified results in the academic system

The Examination Cell acts as the central authority for managing revaluation workflows.

## 7. Evaluation Outcomes and Result Updates

After revaluation or review:

* Marks may remain unchanged
* Marks may be revised based on evaluation findings
* Updated marks are reflected in the academic portal

Revised results replace previous records for academic computation and reporting.

## 8. Impact on Academic Records

Revaluation outcomes directly affect:

* Course grade outcomes
* Academic performance records
* Eligibility for subsequent academic actions

Once updated, revaluation results are treated as final within the academic system.

## 9. Communication of Outcomes

Students are notified of revaluation outcomes through:

* Academic portal notifications
* Official institutional communication channels

Detailed evaluation feedback may not always be provided, depending on the review mechanism used.

## 10. Limitations and Scope

The revaluation process at NCIE:

* Applies only to End-Semester Examination scripts
* Does not modify Continuous Assessment records
* Does not alter examination structure or syllabus coverage

Revaluation focuses strictly on evaluation correctness.

## 11. Special Considerations

Certain academic scenarios may involve additional coordination, such as:

* Final-year students awaiting graduation clearance
* Courses with multiple evaluators
* High-volume revaluation requests

These cases are managed through Examination Cell coordination.

## 12. System Dependencies

The revaluation workflow depends on integration between:

* Examination result databases
* Academic performance records
* Student academic portals
* Evaluation assignment systems

Accurate system synchronization ensures reliable updates.

## 13. Summary

At Nova Crest Institute of Engineering, the revaluation and answer script review process provides a structured academic pathway for students to seek verification of end-semester examination evaluation. Through centralized coordination, controlled workflows, and transparent result updates, NCIE maintains fairness and confidence in its assessment system.
