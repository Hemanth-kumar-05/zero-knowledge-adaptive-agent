Introduction At **Nova Crest Institute of Engineering (NCIE)**, the revaluation and answer script review process provides students with a formal academic mechanism to request a reassessment or review of their evaluated examination scripts. This process supports transparency and confidence in the examination and evaluation system. Revaluation and script review apply **only to End-Semester Examinations (ESE)** and are initiated after the declaration of examination results. This document outlines the workflow, roles involved, and academic practices associated with revaluation at NCIE. ## 2.
